Questions before changing the model

  1. Where exactly is the funnel leaking at each store?
  2. Are low-demo stores capacity constrained or referral constrained?
  3. Are high-demo/low-conversion stores facing objection or qualification issues?
  4. Which promoter practices are associated with higher conversion?
  5. Does store format explain differences, or do exceptions exist?
  6. Which customer objections recur most often?
  7. What happens when store staff actively refer customers?
