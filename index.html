1 · Program overview & diagnostic verdict

What the strategy document says the operation must solve
Operating footprint

31 stores · 48 approved promoter positions

Promoters are deployed across Premium Malls, High Street Opticians and Consumer Electronics outlets to drive product awareness, live demonstrations and unit sales.

  • Top-line volume in the playbook: 92 units sold in Week 1 → 111 in Week 6.
  • Demo volume: 1,184 in Week 1 → 1,476 in Week 6.
  • The playbook frames the issue as moving from assisted-sales activity toward category building.
Diagnostic verdict

Activity is scaling; category-building mechanisms need strengthening

According to the playbook, category building requires clearer daily utility, point-of-sale app activation, sticky optical-staff referral loops and stronger customer retention.

Core friction points
  • ~7.5% overall demo-to-sale conversion in the playbook.
  • Channel performance disparity between High Street optical stores and Premium Malls.
  • Promoter productivity varies by recruitment background.
  • More than 20% of buyers are described as leaving without companion-app pairing/activation.
  • Regular store staff are described as treating promoters as third-party vendors, limiting referrals.
  • Management coverage is described as missing 9–13 store visits weekly.

4 · People capability engine

Peer-to-peer learning from proven selling behaviour
Proposed intervention · People Lab

Peer-to-Peer Promoter Academy / Champion Promoter Program

Use consistently strong promoters as internal trainers. Identify Champions from repeatable performance, codify their selling behaviours, train peers through short role-play sessions, then measure the coached promoter's improvement.

  • Champion selection: consistent sales, conversion, customer feedback, product knowledge, attendance and ability to articulate their method.
  • Training: customer opening, qualification, contextual demos, objection handling, closing and store-team referrals.
  • Measurement: compare baseline vs 2-week and 30-day unit productivity and conversion.
  • Scale: convert proven behaviours into onboarding and the category expansion playbook.

Who becomes a Champion?

Selection should use a balanced scorecard rather than one strong week of sales.

  • Consistent unit sales and demo-to-sale conversion
  • Strong enquiry handling and customer feedback
  • High product knowledge / training performance
  • Reliable attendance and store discipline
  • Ability to explain the “why” behind their selling approach
CHAMPIONTrains 2–3 peers, shares best practices and runs role-play sessions.
COACHED PROMOTERPractices the method, receives shadowing and tracks improvement.
MANAGERValidates the method, observes sessions and monitors performance lift.
PROGRAM TEAMCodifies repeatable practices into the promoter playbook.

What gets taught?

  • Opening: how to approach different customer types.
  • Qualification: identify lifestyle, technology and prescription intent.
  • Demo: make the customer experience the benefit instead of feature-dumping.
  • Objection handling: price, phone comparison, “why do I need it?”, prescription and privacy.
  • Closing: recognise purchase intent and move enquiry → sale.
  • Store collaboration: build referrals from optical/store staff.
